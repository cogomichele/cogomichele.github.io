I am an Assistant Professor of Fluid Dynamics at the University of Padova, in the Department of Industrial Engineering, where I am part of the [FLUMS](https://research.dii.unipd.it/flums/) group.

My research is on the aerothermodynamics of high-speed wall-bounded flows. I use direct numerical simulation and large-eddy simulation on GPU-accelerated solvers to study compressible turbulent boundary layers — how they respond to Mach number, wall cooling, and surface roughness — and I develop the wall models that bring those flows within reach of engineering-cost computation.
