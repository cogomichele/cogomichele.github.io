Looking for a thesis?

I supervise MSc theses in aerospace engineering on high-speed aerodynamics, turbulence simulation and wall modelling. Projects range from analysis of existing simulation databases — which needs no supercomputing time and suits students who want depth without a systems-heavy workload — through to full DNS campaigns on European Tier-0 machines.

If you are interested, get in touch by email with a short note about your background and what appeals to you.
